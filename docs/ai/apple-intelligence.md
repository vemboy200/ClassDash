# Apple Intelligence

Apple's own model, built into macOS. It runs on your Mac with nothing to install or pay for. It's the simplest AI to set up, but also the most limited: it can't look things up, so it only answers about what it's given.

## Before you start

- A Mac with Apple silicon, macOS 26 or later, and Apple Intelligence turned on in System Settings.

## Setting it up

Nothing to install: Settings → **AI** → **Apple Intelligence** shows it ready once Apple Intelligence is on. Its **Test** button checks that ClassDash can reach it and get an answer.

## What it can do

- Read announcements for homework.
- Answer in the workspace's AI pane about the PDF page you're on.

## Privacy

Everything stays on your computer: nothing ClassDash asks it goes anywhere else.

## Known limitations

- It can't look things up: no assignments, instructions, announcements or other PDFs while answering, only the page it's given.
- It can't see images, and has no thinking setting.
- Mac only.

## Troubleshooting

- **Not ready:** check that the Mac has Apple silicon and macOS 26 or later, and that Apple Intelligence is on in System Settings.

## Turning it off

Pick another AI in the picker (under the question box in the AI pane or the Grades center, and **Read by** under **Announcements**). ClassDash never runs it on its own.
