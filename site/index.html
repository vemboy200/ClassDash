What's in the box

More than a to-do list

Your assignments are the start. ClassDash adds the tools around them.

Dashboard

Everything due, checked by itself during the school day, with the teacher's instructions and announcements. A notification only when something changes.

Grades

Your grade in every class, its history, alerts when it changes, and an estimate from the category weights.

Calendar and schedule

No-school days and events from your school's calendar PDF or any calendar feed, plus A/B or odd/even class days, so you know what's tomorrow.

Workspace

Write on worksheet PDFs and export them ready to hand in, with Google Docs or Desmos open beside them.

AI helper, if you want one

Bring your own, even one running on your own computer. It helps you get there yourself instead of doing the work. Off until you set it up.

Home Assistant and API

Put your schoolwork on a Home Assistant dashboard, or anything else on your home network.

Your data

It stays on your computer

ClassDash has no server or account of its own, and the person who made it never sees your data. It's open source, so anyone can check.

  • Your account, your computerIt signs in as you, in a browser on your computer, and sees exactly what you can already see.
  • Read-onlyIt never turns in, answers, changes or deletes anything. Neither can the AI.
  • No trackingNo analytics, no ads, no account to make. Your Google password goes into Google's own sign-in page, never into ClassDash.

It's unofficial

ClassDash isn't made or approved by Google, Instructure, Aequitas, Edpuzzle or your school. Canvas has an official API it can use. For the others, it reads the same pages you see when you're signed in.

That means it can break when a site changes, and your school's rules about automated access still apply. We'd rather say so up front.
